DataBase for Khayate

A specialized management system for tailors and fashion houses to manage customer measurements and orders efficiently.

Project Overview

The Khayate Database (Tailoring System) is designed to digitize the manual record-keeping of tailoring shops. It focuses on precision, order tracking, and maintaining detailed customer profiles.

Key Features

  • Measurement History Tracking
  • Order Status Workflow
  • Automated Invoicing
  • Searchable Customer Database
