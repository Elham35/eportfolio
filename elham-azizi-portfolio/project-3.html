University MIS

A Management Information System concept designed for university academic data and reporting.

Project Overview

The University MIS centralizes academic records, student enrollment, and departmental reporting into a single, unified interface for university administrators.

Key Features

  • Student Lifecycle Management
  • Departmental Analytics
  • Role-based Access (RBAC)
  • Automated Grade Reports
