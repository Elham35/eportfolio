University Journal

A PHP/XAMPP publication system for managing academic papers and university journals.

Project Overview

This platform simplifies the process of academic publishing. Authors can submit papers, reviewers can provide feedback, and editors can manage the entire publication cycle.

Key Features

  • Paper Submission System
  • Automated Email Notifications
  • File Upload & Management
  • Searchable Journal Archive
